The A-10 Warthog's GAU-8 Avenger cannon pushes back with about 10,000 pounds of average recoil, slightly more than the 9,065 pounds of thrust from one of the jet's two engines. The plane was designed around this gun, and it's mounted off-center for a reason.

Key numbers
• GAU-8/A Avenger: 30 mm, 7 barrels, about 20 feet long (U.S. Air Force Test Center)
• Rate of fire: 3,900 rounds a minute (USAF)
• Ammo drum: up to 1,174 rounds; a typical load is about 1,150
• Gun + feed system + full drum: about 4,029 lb, nearly two tons
• Average recoil: about 10,000 lbf vs 9,065 lb of thrust per TF34 engine (USAF fact sheet)
• A burst slows the jet by only a few mph in level flight
• Mounting: gun offset to the left so the firing barrel sits on the centerline; nose landing gear offset to the right

Our math (Apex Reports)
• ~1,150 rounds ÷ 3,900 rounds/min ≈ 17.7 seconds of total firing time, which is why pilots fire short 1-2 second bursts

Caveats: Recoil is an average figure from the gun's maker; the A-10 has two engines, so total thrust is about 18,000 lb. The original gun could be set to 2,100 or 4,200 rounds a minute before being fixed at 3,900. Figures are rounded.
